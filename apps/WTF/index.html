Costing Notes & Assumptions
How each number was derived
▼
Initial assumed portion: 100g chicken + 120g French fries, with sauces, seasoning and coating.

The quantities you previously sent (Marinade 12, Peri Peri 40, Mayo 24, etc.) were used as purchased stock quantities, because they reconcile with your ₹25,640 food-stock bill.
Parcel covers: Your purchase is ₹180 per 1 kg. Since the number of covers in 1 kg is unknown, enter the actual pieces/kg in the packaging section.

Dustbin covers: These are treated as an operating expense, NOT as packaging consumed by each customer.
Packaging calculation: Black parcel box + lid = ₹400 / 100 = ₹4.00 each.

Brown bowl = ₹420 / 100 = ₹4.20 each.

Plate = ₹110 / 100 = ₹1.10 each.

Fork = ₹50 / 500 = ₹0.10 each.

Tissue = ₹20 / 500 = ₹0.04 each.

Parcel cover = ₹180 / 1kg. Its per-piece cost depends on the actual number of covers obtained from 1kg.

The calculator therefore does NOT assume a parcel-cover price until you enter pieces/kg.

The ₹70 value previously mentioned is completely excluded from this calculator.
